## Overcoming Local Soil Moisture Challenges with GPS-Guided Drain Tile

Drain tile systems in the Red River Valley depend on precise installation for effective results. We use modern tile plows with RTK GPS grade control to install tile at precise depths and grades, even across complex terrain.

These devices collect real-time data to make adjustments during drain tile installation to provide exact, centimeter-level positioning to prevent over- or under-draining (crucial for success in fine-textured soils).

The unmatched accuracy produced by RTK GPS helps ensure optimal slope design for the natural contours of the land. This can lead to improved water management that prevents pooling and erosion. Real-time accuracy also supports faster installation, reducing downtime and the need for adjustments.
