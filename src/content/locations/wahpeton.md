## Tailored Drainage Systems for Wahpeton Soil

In most cases, the drainage systems can vary from one field to the next depending on the needs and the soil.

One major challenge with local soil is the presence of a sodium-rich layer that hardens into a surface crust when exposed to air. Our tile solutions are specifically tailored to address these conditions, paired with [services](/services) that manage sodium levels to keep drainage flowing smoothly. Because this crust varies in thickness across fields, we carefully adjust installation depth and grading to match the precise soil layers throughout your project.

Once we start the installation process, you could see a variety of these on your farm to help us get the drain to the correct spot and ensure it is graded properly. You'll not only have an improved water system for drainage, but you'll see the health of your soil improve, also.
