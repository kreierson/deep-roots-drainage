## Overcoming the Unique Farmland Drainage Challenges of Cass County

The Valley is known for heavy clay soils, which drain very slowly. When the soil becomes saturated with water, it tends to stay saturated for a long time. If the soil is too wet in the spring, farmers are not able to plant as early as they want to. If the soil becomes saturated after the crops are already in, the wet soil can asphyxiate the root systems. Drain tile installation in the heavy clay helps manage the water tables, so the soil is ideal for planting and growing.

The dense clay soil found throughout farmland in the Red River Valley is often susceptible to the development of saline spots. High in sodium, this soil becomes hard and crusty at the surface. Deep Roots Drainage addresses these problem areas, flushing salt away from the root zone, allowing crops to grow successfully.
