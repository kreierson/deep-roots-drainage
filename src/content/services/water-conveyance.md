## The Crucial Role of Water Conveyance in Agricultural Drainage

Water conveyance systems are the networks of ditches, channels, and waterways that transport collected water away from agricultural fields and direct it safely into regional watersheds. Without proper water conveyance, tile lines can back up, causing waterlogged soils and defeating the entire purpose of your drainage investment.

Effective water conveyance ensures that water moves efficiently off your property without causing soil erosion or pooling in low-lying areas.

At Deep Roots Drainage, we focus on maximizing efficiency so that subsurface drainage can function at maximum capacity. When downstream channels are restricted, the entire drainage infrastructure suffers, leading to reduced crop yields and delayed field access.
